# Dr Omar Ali Al-Najras Clinic — Website and Mobile Application

**Stage: Website and Android app listing available**

I created a website and Android application for Dr Omar Ali Al-Najras's dental clinic. They connect information about the doctor and clinic services with appointment and contact entry points, using Arabic, English and Turkish interfaces. This is a separate project from Sinad.

The current website at [alnajrasclinic.com](https://alnajrasclinic.com/) opens at a sign-in screen. That destination is linked as account access, not as an unrestricted preview of the clinic's internal workflows.

The [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yahyaalali.dental_clinic_app) identifies the clinic app and YAHYA ALALI as its developer. Account-based functions require sign-in; no patient records or real bookings are displayed in this case study.
