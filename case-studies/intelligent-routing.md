# Intelligent Routing and Quality of Service in SDN–MPLS Networks

**Stage: Ongoing doctoral research**

When network load changes or a link fails, a previously suitable route may no longer be the best choice. I study how routing decisions can respond to these changes in software-defined networks using MPLS, and how their effects on quality of service can be evaluated.

My work links network observations, a representation of the current state and programmable forwarding. PPO is the routing-decision component; LSTM-based prediction is being studied as an auxiliary input, not as a replacement for the routing decision. Control instructions program the forwarding devices while user packets remain on the network's data paths.

![Conceptual alternate and selected network paths with separate control signals; not an experimental result](../assets/editorial/routing-paths.svg)

I develop the experimental environment, measurement-to-decision integration and evaluation method. Research is ongoing; comparisons and statistical validation must be completed before presenting results.
