# Ashara — A City Portal

**Stage: Live city website; governorate expansion planned**

Ashara brings city information and public service entry points into one website. Its visitor-facing interface is available at [ashara-city.com](https://ashara-city.com/).

I develop the application and its integration with the backend. The project combines Flutter interfaces with Django services and distinguishes public information from authenticated administrative work.

The next development direction is expansion to the wider Deir Ezzor governorate. This is separate from the municipal operations platform; a live city website does not establish that the planned expansion is already in service.
