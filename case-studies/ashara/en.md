## The city portal

I developed Ashara to bring city information and service entry points into one interface. Visitors can browse news and public information, check weather and prayer times, and find the relevant service. Account-based services lead into the sign-in journey.

My work covers the application interface and backend services. Flutter presents the information, while Django services handle operations and data. Development focuses on clear journeys between information and services, with access suited to the visitor or signed-in user.

## Available now

Visit [Ashara](https://ashara-city.com/) to explore the online website and its public service interface. Its functions and user experience continue to develop. The displayed screenshot is from the actual public interface, not an administration dashboard or proof of every account-based service.

## The next development scope

The portal began with Ashara and is being developed towards a Deir Ezzor governorate-wide experience, with organised space for each city and its content. This requires a city-based information and service structure that makes the relevant destination clear to visitors.

The portal is separate from the [municipal administration platform](https://yahyaalali.com/en/content-library/project/deir-ezzor-municipalities/), which organises administrative work, tasks and projects across governorates and municipalities.
