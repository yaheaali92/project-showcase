## A clinic-specific experience

I developed Dr Omar Ali Al-Najras Clinic's website and Android app as entry points to information about the doctor, clinic services and contact options. The web presence connects with a mobile app, with Arabic, English and Turkish interfaces.

The app listing describes doctor and service information, news, appointment access and a patient account. Account-based functions require sign-in; this case study displays no patient records or real bookings.
